Food is wasted in one lane while hunger waits in the next.

Kitchens cook for the crowd that might come. Banquet halls over-order. Households buy more than they eat. Meanwhile, millions skip meals, and the two sides rarely meet.

Roughly one third of all food produced for people is lost or wasted. The dashed plates show that share.

~78 milliontonnes of household food wasted in India each year (UNEP Food Waste Index 2024)
~1 in 3plates of food produced worldwide never gets eaten (FAO)
~200 millionpeople in India are undernourished (FAO, State of Food Security)

One platform. Donors on one side, NGOs on the other.

FoodBridge does the hard part in the middle: matching surplus to the nearest verified NGO, checking food safety details, and sending a volunteer to collect it.

For donors

Post what you have in under a minute. We handle the rest.

For NGOs and kitchens

See nearby donations in real time and accept what fits your day’s menu.

For neighbours

Volunteer for a pickup slot or run a collection drive in your society.

From surplus to supper in four steps

  1. List the food

    Add the type, quantity, packing and how long it stays fresh.

  2. Get matched

    The nearest NGO with capacity is alerted and confirms.

  3. Pickup is arranged

    A volunteer driver collects it within your chosen window.

  4. See your impact

    You get a receipt with meals served and food saved.

Built around real kitchens and real pickups

Easy food donation

A listing form made for a busy kitchen pass. Repeat donors can re-post yesterday’s listing in one tap, and surplus alerts nudge you before closing time.

Verified NGO partners

Every partner is checked for registration, storage and serving capacity.

Pickup coordination

Live volunteer tracking, time windows and simple handover codes.

Community participation

Residents’ associations, student groups and neighbours can sign up for pickup shifts, host collection points and track their area’s totals together.
